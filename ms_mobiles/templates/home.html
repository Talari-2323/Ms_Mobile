FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our mobile repair service.

How can I book a mobile repair?
Go to the Book Repair section, enter your customer and mobile details and submit the repair request.
How do I track my repair?
After booking, you will receive a unique Repair ID. Enter that ID in the Track Repair section to check the repair status.
How is the repair cost decided?
The mobile is diagnosed first. The owner can discuss the estimated cost with the customer before the repair proceeds.
Do you provide pickup and delivery?
Yes. A delivery agent / mediator can coordinate mobile pickup and delivery.
Which mobile brands do you service?
MS Mobiles can support major brands such as Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, Vivo, Oppo, Xiaomi and other smartphones.
Can I communicate about repair cost?
Yes. The communication section is designed for discussing repair problems, estimated cost, final cost and customer approval.
What mobile repair services do you provide?
MS Mobiles provides screen replacement, battery replacement, charging repair, camera repair, speaker repair, software troubleshooting and other mobile servicing.
How long does mobile repair take?
Most common mobile repairs can be completed within 2–3 hours. Major repairs or repairs requiring special parts may take 1–2 days.
Do you replace mobile screens and batteries?
Yes. MS Mobiles provides mobile screen and battery replacement services. Availability and cost depend on the mobile model.
What should I do if my phone has water damage?
Switch off the mobile and bring it to MS Mobiles for inspection. Our team can check the device and identify the possible water-related damage.
